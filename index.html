01 / THE IDEA

Choose a region.
Hear the voices within it.

Regional extraction turns distance into a control: the target is the sum of every speaker closer than the query radius.

yr(t) = ∑i : dᵢ < r sᵢ(t)Strict boundary: a speaker enters only when dᵢ < r.

Abstract · reading summary

This work studies monaural regional speech extraction with a continuously adjustable query radius. Continuous radius conditioning lets one model select different spatial target regions, while cross-radius consistency learning encourages stable predictions when two queries include the same speakers. Experiments investigate full-target preservation, partial-target extraction, empty-target rejection, and behavior under additional speakers and noise.

02 / THE METHOD

Learn what changes.
Constrain what stays.

The same mixture is evaluated at different radius queries. Training consistency is enforced on unchanged, non-empty target sets.

Training objective & implementation details

Four queries, one mixture

Training samples one radius in each of [0,1), [1,2), [2,3), [3,4) m. Validation uses 1/2/3/4 m. EXP6 is initialized from the discrete best48 backbone with a new radius encoder; it also uses reweighted training profiles.

Consistency, not a boundary detector

L = Lext + 0.1 Lcon
Lcon = meanvalid pairs MAE(ŷa − ŷb) / RMS(ya)

Only adjacent queries with identical non-empty targets and target RMS > 10⁻⁴ are valid. No valid pair gives zero consistency loss. Extraction uses SNRLP, a 40 cap with straight-through gradients, and 100 × MAE(output) for zero targets.

Why no RCE headline or “best model” badge?

The draft mixes aggregation rules. Its 0.81% and 0.85% RCE values match medians of unchanged-target pairs, not the mean specified by its equation. The final-model 0.72% has no confirmed source. RCE is withheld here until the checkpoint, radius grid, valid-pair rule and aggregation are fixed. The current results do not establish an across-the-board best model.
